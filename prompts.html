Passport size prompts

I have uploaded a selfie photo taken with a phone. Please transform it into a professional Tanzania passport size photo that meets official requirements. Do the following: 1. Upscale and sharpen the image to high resolution (at least 600 DPI equivalent quality) — fix any blur, noise, or low quality from the phone camera. 2. Change the background to a plain, solid light blue color (#ADD8E6 or similar soft passport blue) — remove and replace the original background completely and cleanly. 3. Crop and reframe the image to the Tanzania passport photo standard: 35mm × 45mm portrait ratio, with the face centered, eyes at approximately the upper third of the frame, and the top of the head leaving a small gap from the top edge. 4. Make the face look natural and well-lit — correct any harsh shadows, uneven skin tone, or unflattering phone lighting so the photo looks like it was taken in a professional photo studio. 5. The subject must have a neutral expression, eyes open and clearly visible, mouth closed, and be looking directly at the camera. 6. Do NOT add glasses, hats, face coverings, or any accessories that were not in the original photo. 7. The final image must look like an official, print-ready Tanzania passport photograph that would be accepted at the immigration office. Output a clean, high-quality passport photo ready for printing or official submission.
